01

Fortune Teller App

JULY 2026

A manga café and community space was running reduced hours over the summer heat and needed extra income. It also wanted to try fundraising as a yearly tradition. I designed and built a mobile-first omikuji app in React. Guests drew a physical fortune card (Star, Moon, Cloud, or Sakura), scanned its QR code, and saved a randomized fortune to their phones. Assets were developed on Canva and I curated the fortune database with the café team. The event raised $500+, and its reusable assets are ready for next year.

e3 Website

JUNE 2026

Client since 2022. I first built a2z’s brand, Wix site, and social campaigns. 03

Transformative Student Voice

2025

Transformative Student Voice is a Denver-based group of educators who help schools give students a platform. They were launching two books that collected years of their work and research, and their student-designed website no longer matched that milestone. I refreshed the Wix site’s visual design and branding, and created a brand style guide so future contributors could keep it consistent. While updating the site, I found that a single student held all admin access. I flagged the risk and set up a permissions process so incoming students and staff could keep running the site securely.

01

Safety Badges

PRODUCT DESIGN

As a digital nomad who walks a lot at night, I couldn’t find visibility wear that was actually stylish. So I made it. Inspired by Japanese street fashion and cyberpunk, I designed reflective wooden badges that clip on and off bags, hats, and jackets. I partnered with Mapache Design: I led the concept, vision board, designs, and production files, and Mapache handled laser fabrication. I built the process to be replicable so their Philadelphia team could scale it while I traveled. The badges debuted as prizes at the Tanabata fundraiser.

02

Rolling Through Space | SAIKA

CHARACTER ASSETS

Hired as illustrator, I stepped into project coordination when SAIKA’s Fan Expo plans changed two months out. The team was split between a fantasy and a sci-fi TTRPG, so I proposed building on Rolling Through Space, a staff member’s existing sci-fi space exploration game, and expanding it to support both. I designed the races, classes, villain tokens, and Star Trek–inspired uniforms and badges, and helped oversee production of the physical assets with Mapache Salvage Design.

Monster Babes

03 / Music Clients

Art made for the music.

Artwork and visual concepts created with independent musicians.

01

Amalgam Effect

ALBUM ART

Visual art for a Denver prog-rock band, starting in 2014. I illustrated and digitized the band’s chimera mascot, with each head representing a member (nine-tailed fox, lion, anaconda, goat, and eagle). I hand-painted the covers for a three-album series in acrylic on canvas. The albums followed the stories of the fictional character Alan Quinn. I also did the digital art for their “Hocus Pocus” cover single, and a poster for a BLM tribute concert. All of the albums are released and streaming.

Dakota Slim

ALBUM ART & IDENTITY

Digital cover art for trap artist Dakota Slim, from 2022 to 2023.
